Labels (follow-up vocabulary; the three added columns and rows 29–30 use only these four):
- `EXPLICIT` — stated in the source.
- `PARTIAL / IMPLIED` — follows from the source's formulas only after algebra and assumptions the source does not
  supply (written out in the linked entry and in `B5_MATH_COMPARISON.md`).
- `RELATED BUT DIFFERENT` — a related statement about a different object, statistic, ensemble, estimator or
  measurement.
- `NOT LOCATED` — not found in the reviewed version after the logged searches. This is **not** a statement about the
  wider literature.

Legacy label in the Thanasilp and Aghaei Saem columns: `PARTIAL / RELATED` (initial B5 round) means a related but
non-identical statement, i.e. the same role as `RELATED BUT DIFFERENT`. No initial-round cell was relabelled.

added in the follow-up audit. Rows 31–33 were added in the closure audit for the principal track's candidate
